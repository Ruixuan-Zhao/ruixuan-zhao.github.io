About

Ruixuan Zhao

Postdoctoral Fellow

ruixuan.zhao@utoronto.ca GitHub

I am a Postdoctoral Fellow in the Department of Computer & Mathematical Sciences at the University of Toronto, supported by the CANSSI Ontario Postdoctoral Fellowship in Statistical Sciences (2025–2027). My research focuses on causal inference and graphical models, with a particular interest in causal estimands for clinical trials.

I received my Ph.D. in Data Science from the Department of Data Science at City University of Hong Kong in 2024, with support from the Hong Kong PhD Fellowship Scheme. Previously, I earned an M.S. in Statistics from the Department of Statistics at the University of Wisconsin–Madison and a B.S. in Statistics from the School of Mathematics and Statistics at Wuhan University.
